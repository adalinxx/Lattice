## Feeds: 51%-attack-cost / security-budget model C5)

Two distinct thresholds fall out of the curves and must be fed to C5 separately — conflating them silently over-states the security budget:

- **Majority threshold (deep-reorg safety): f > 50%.** Out-working a `24`-deep honest segment becomes likely above majority.
- **Balancing liveness cost:** full-horizon survival scales as f^horizon in this model; it does not introduce a fixed 50% threshold.
- **Economic threshold (selfish-mining profitability): f = 1/4 = 25%.** This is the *lower* economic-security bound and the binding one for the security budget. It is the analytic Eyal–Sirer crossing R(f, γ=1/2) = f at the γ = 1/2 the fork choice measures — well below majority — so a rational attacker has a revenue incentive to deviate at the classic threshold long before it can reorg or stall the chain.

C5 must price the security budget against the **lower** of the two — the selfish-mining economic threshold — not the 50% majority point. The budget is the honest root-grind cost required to keep an attacker below that fraction of this chain's verified work stream; another chain's canonical history cannot change work already admitted here.
